This post is about why I started writing a blog in the first place. Recently I've been growing more and more overwhelmed by the design of the modern-day internet. It is true that the internet has made the impossible possible; just take a look, everybody has made friends they couldn't otherwise imagine, we can find our likes through the social media. I too, growing up in the era of internet, and always being the "weird tech guy" I am, have been relying on the internet for all of my social interactions throughout my whole life.

However, there were some occasions that made me reconsider my internet usage. Don't mistaken me, I am not saying that "I hate the whole technology we should go back to stone age", no, I am not that type of guy. Well, you should continue to read to understand me, unfortunately there's no TL;DR.

## The Internet Blackout in Iran

Let me begin with the first half of 2026. In Iran, 2026 began with protests, suppression, war, etc.
And, expectedly, the internet was shut down the whole internet for a very long time.
I don't remember exactly but I think we didn't have access to the internet for _three to four months_!
We had only access to some kind of intranet—network within a country.

Well, that said, I couldn't communicate with any of my online friends, and my closest friends live far away from me. That said, I wanted some way to connect with them. Nothing special of course, I just wanted to talk to them, maybe share pictures, etc. Anyhting that can't happen without SMS.

Long story short, with helo of my friends, I self-hosted a [Matrix](https://matrix.org) server only for my own social circle, and I gotta say, it was way better that what I had experienced on Telegram or _any other social media_:

- **There were my friends**. The social media had a clear purpose. No channels, no posts, no stories. You only used it to talk to the ones you actually wanted to talk to.
- **E2E Encrypted!**: Well, it is a little irrelevant, but I could stop worrying my data being leaked by a company employee.
- **No Channels**: I don't hate channels, I hate seeing channels and chats in one single app. Although the idea of a _public room_ is close, it's different. Essentially, I liked the idea of talking directly instead of sending something in my daily and waiting for somebody to reply.
- **No Bloat**: To be honest, Matrix did not look like a _Chinese Super App_.

Of course we had lost a lot of things, because of the internet. But during that time I had a very intimate time with my friends. And I really liked this part of that time.

## Emails & Blogs!

### My Friend Who Withdrew from the Internet

Well, recently, one of my close friends completely withdrew from any type of social media,
and I only have her email address, and that's it, no IDs, no phone numbers, nothing.
She also stopped using internet during the day, and only uses it during nights. It's basically a detoxification.
We've been communicating through email, and at first, I thought maybe that's gonna make us forget each other.
But surprisingly, this correspedence feels much better than I expected. This kinda resembles physical letters;
and I love sending and receiving physical letters. The great thing about them is that it takes time to arrive,
so in the meanwhile while the mail is on the way, your ideas accumulate and you'll have a much more pleasant conversation.
But talking directly and constantly using the phone... yeah that's gonna exhust me. That's why I love emails and delayed responses. And of course, I always wanted to find a _Kiriko_, like the one from _Itai no itai no tonde yuke—Sugrau Miaki_.

### The Blogs Era

Thanks to my father, I started writing blogs on generic weblog sites as soon as I was 7.
I have a very nostalgic and good memory from that time.
Everything seemed easier, newer, happier. Back then, I saw people on forums discussing, started making programmer
friends on forums, wrote blogs no one ever read, just like now.
Compared to the bustling and aggressive atmosphere of Twitter
(I last used it back then when it was Twitter), that was a way calmer, tranquil time for me.

Hence, I decided to give it one more try. I don't know if anyone is gonna ever read any of it, but I am hoping so. I am doing this
to find interesting people, although, on a second thought...
how are you gonna message me even if you're interested? Well, use my email please.

## I Hate Talking to LLMs!

Well, as weird as it sounds, I spend a lot of time venting to LLMs, about my daily life,
or even explain scientific/mathematical topics for them, only because I am bored.
Why not tell them on a blog instead? Or even about the movies I find interesting.
That way I might find real people who share my taste.

# What I Want To Do

Basically, from now on, I want to reduce my social media consumption if I can.
It's gonna be impossible if I want to completely scrap them, since I have friends I don't want to lose,
and not everybody is going to exchange emails with me,
but I want to recreate that "Matrix Server" era for myself.

So, that's why I would:

- Reduce my screen time on phone, use PC instead (technically, a phone is still a _personal computer_ though).
- Write on this blog instead of talking to an AI instead.
